LEVERS WORK AS ON/OFF TOGGLES. Remember this! 
Previous lever positions remain in effect the whole day/uptime. 
 
Pull the appropriate lever as many times as indicated above, then move to 
lower-case letter which teleports you to the next lever. Wash, rinse, repeat. 
 
A wrong lever pull will keep you from going through that alley branch. Your 
attempt to exit the branch will teleport you to the #1, where you must go 
north through T2 to the % and start lever alley again. When you get to the 
lever that misdirected you to the #1, toggle the lever (Pull lever once.) 
Continue. 
 
Landing on a [#1] (going the wrong way) will teleport+poison you to #1 above. 
 
The #9 branch exit (J) teleports to a room west of 4SW (j). The 4SW room HAS 
NO WEST EXIT. You must kill the 4th warrior to get the key to open the door to 
the east. Or you must already have a key from a previous venture through 
Level 6+. East east, zoom zoom. 
 
4SW: 4 Stone Warriors. They have two hits - Kick for 400, Area damage 
for up to 200 with possibility of tumbling out of the room, and direct-damage 
up to 200 with chance of multiple-round-lasting paralysis. Defeating this first 
set gives you the Ornate Stone Key (opens # door east). 
 
Stone Keys are obtainable in Level 6 & Level 8
